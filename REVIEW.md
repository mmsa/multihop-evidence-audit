# Independent evidence review
For each question, decide whether the proposed answer follows using only the two supplied passages and their titles. You may use ordinary language understanding, arithmetic and date comparison. Use no web search, outside factual knowledge or other reader's judgements. Assess each item independently even when a question repeats.

Enter one lowercase verdict:
- single: at least one passage is sufficient by itself. Quote the decisive wording.
- joint: both passages are needed and together establish all required links. Quote each link.
- insufficient: a specific fact or connection is missing, the entity is wrong, or the evidence contradicts the proposed answer. Identify the problem.
- indeterminate: ambiguity prevents a reliable decision even after close reading. Explain why.

Check entity identity, qualifiers, dates and comparisons. The proposed answer appearing in the passage is insufficient by itself. Accept equivalent names when the text establishes identity. Titles may identify the subject. Do not infer a relationship simply because two names co-occur. Put exact excerpts in evidence_quotes and your explanation in reason. Preserve item IDs and all source text.
